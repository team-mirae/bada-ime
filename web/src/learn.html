    Learn to type Korean

    1. Three levels. Level 1 teaches the Hangul letters by typing them in bada roman (Korean spelled in Latin letters). Level 2 adds its shortcuts. Level 3 is 2-set, the standard Korean keyboard. Jump to any lesson whenever you like.
    2. Type a word, then Space. Above each word are its standard romanization and the keys to press; turn either off to test yourself. The next key lights up on the keyboard.
    3. Several readings? The first shows as you type; Tab or ↓ moves to the next one in the list, then Space commits it.
    4. Everything runs in your browser, and your progress stays there.
